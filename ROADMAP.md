# Roadmap

Reiko's Godzilla Mod is built in five phases. Each phase is playable on its own and adds to the systems before it.

Target: **Minecraft 1.21.1 · NeoForge · GeckoLib 4**

---

## Phase 1: "King of the Monsters" core

The best-known monsters, plus the systems every later monster reuses.

**Monsters**
- [ ] Godzilla: Showa (1954–75) and Heisei (1984–95) variants
- [ ] King Ghidorah (three independent gravity beams, flight, wing gusts)
- [ ] Mothra: larva (silk spray) → cocoon → imago (poison scales, hurricane gusts)
- [ ] Rodan (supersonic flight, sonic-boom dive)
- [ ] Anguirus (spiked shell, Thunderball roll, tameable and rideable)
- [ ] Mechagodzilla, Showa (Space Beam, finger missiles, force field, "Fake Godzilla" disguise)

**Tech and items**
- [ ] Type 66 Maser Tank (drivable; unmanned tanks auto-fire at hostile kaiju)
- [ ] Oxygen Destroyer (endgame weapon)
- [ ] Kaiju drops: G-Cells, Space Titanium, Ghidorah Scales, Mothra Scales, Kaiju Scales

**World**
- [ ] Monster Island (volcano, Godzilla, Rodan, Anguirus lairs)
- [ ] Infant Island (jungle, shrine, Mothra Egg)
- [ ] Kaiju events: Godzilla surfaces offshore, Ghidorah meteor, Mechagodzilla invasion

**Core systems**
- [ ] Charged beam attacks (raycast, block damage throttle, rendered beams)
- [ ] Flight AI for flying kaiju
- [ ] Multipart hitboxes
- [ ] Faction AI (Titans / Guardians / Invaders)
- [ ] Configurable scale (default 1/5 film size) and anti-grief settings
- [ ] Boss bars

## Phase 2: Heisei

- Biollante (Flower Beast and Plant Beast)
- Battra (larva and imago)
- Heisei Mechagodzilla + Garuda → Super Mechagodzilla
- Fire Rodan (Uranium Heat Beam, energy transfer → Godzilla's Red Spiral Ray)
- SpaceGodzilla (Corona Beam, crystal fields)
- MOGUERA (splits into Star Falcon and Land Moguera)
- Destoroyah (full evolution line, Micro → Perfect Form)
- Baby → Little → Junior Godzilla
- Burning Godzilla meltdown state
- Super X, Super X2 (Fire Mirror), Super X3, cadmium missiles, freezer tanks
- **New system:** heat and freeze meter (weakness system)

## Phase 3: MonsterVerse

- MonsterVerse Godzilla (blue → red thermonuclear → evolved pink)
- Kong (Battle Axe, B.E.A.S.T. Glove)
- MUTOs (EMP), Skullcrawlers, Warbats
- MonsterVerse King Ghidorah (Alpha Call event)
- Scylla, Behemoth, Methuselah, Tiamat
- MonsterVerse Mechagodzilla (Proton Scream)
- Skar King, Shimo, Suko
- Orca device, HEAV, Hollow Earth dimension

## Phase 4: Showa and Millennium deep cuts

- Gigan, Hedorah, Megalon, Jet Jaguar, Ebirah, Kumonga, Kamacuras
- Minilla, Gorosaurus, Manda, Baragon, Varan, King Caesar, Titanosaurus
- Orga, Megaguirus, GMK roster, Kiryu, Monster X → Keizer Ghidorah, Zilla
- Gotengo, Dimension Tide

## Phase 5: Reiwa and Modern

- Shin Godzilla (four forms + Yashiori event)
